Ultimate Video Games is a collection tracker for video game collectors.

Keep track of your games, consoles and accessories in one place. Organise your collection, find what you own, add your own information, and keep your collection backed up.

Download on the App Store

⸻

🎮 Your Collection, Organised

Ultimate Video Games is designed to make managing a growing game collection simple.

* 🎮 Track your games — Keep a record of the games in your collection.
* 🕹️ Track consoles — Keep your consoles and hardware organised.
* 🎧 Track accessories — Record controllers, cables, peripherals and other accessories.
* 🔎 Search & filter — Quickly find items in your collection.
* ✏️ Custom information — Add your own details and information to items.
* 📥 Import collections — Import your existing collection into the app.
* 💾 Backups — Create backups of your collection so your data is safe.
* 🔐 No account required — There is no login or account system.
* 💳 No subscription — No subscription or in-app purchase is required.

🔒 Your Collection Is Yours

Ultimate Video Games does not require you to create an account or sign in.

There are:

* No accounts
* No login system
* No subscription
* No required in-app purchases

The app is designed to let you manage your collection without needing an online account.

🆘 Support

Found a bug, need help, or have an idea for a new feature?

Please open an issue in this repository.

When reporting a problem, it helps to include:

* The device you are using
* Your iOS, iPadOS or macOS version
* Your version of Ultimate Video Games
* What you were trying to do
* What happened
* Any error message displayed

Before submitting an issue

Please avoid including sensitive information such as:

* Passwords
* API credentials
* Private collection data
* Personal information
* Other sensitive data

If you would prefer to contact me directly, you can email:

support@william.me.uk

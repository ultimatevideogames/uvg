# Privacy Policy — Ultimate Video Games

Last updated: 23/07/2026

## What data the app stores

Ultimate Video Games stores the collection data you enter — game 
titles, platforms, cover images, and any custom fields you add. This 
data is stored on your device and, if you have iCloud enabled, is 
synced to your other devices using Apple's CloudKit, under your own 
iCloud account. This data is stored in your own private iCloud 
storage — we do not operate any servers, and we do not have access 
to your collection data.

## Third-party services (optional)

The app can optionally connect to third-party services — Steam, eBay, 
RAWG, TheGamesDB, and GameSpot — to help you look up game covers 
and details. These integrations only activate if you choose to enter 
your own API credentials for these services in Settings. When enabled, 
search queries you make are sent directly to that service using your 
own account/credentials, under that service's own privacy policy. We 
do not receive, see, or store this traffic.

## What we don't do

- We do not collect analytics or usage data
- We do not use advertising or tracking
- We do not have user accounts or a backend server
- We do not sell or share your data with anyone
